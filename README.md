# HR Attrition Analytics | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

HR Attrition Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and identify workforce patterns that may influence employee retention.

The dashboard provides a visual analysis of employee demographics, turnover patterns, attrition drivers, and employee wellness indicators.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- HR Analytics

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of employee count, attrition, attrition rate, and major workforce trends.

### 2. Employee Demographics
Analyzes employee demographics such as:
- Age
- Gender
- Education
- Marital Status
- Distance From Home

### 3. Turnover Analysis
Examines attrition patterns across:
- Departments
- Job Roles
- Business Travel
- Overtime
- Monthly Income
- Years in Current Role

### 4. Attrition Drivers
Explores factors associated with employee attrition, including:
- Job Level
- Overtime
- Performance Rating
- Monthly Income
- Employee Tenure

### 5. Employee Wellness
Examines workplace and employee satisfaction indicators such as:
- Environment Satisfaction
- Job Satisfaction
- Job Involvement
- Relationship Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to transform HR employee data into meaningful visual insights that can help organizations better understand attrition patterns and identify areas where employee retention may need attention.

## 📈 Key Areas of Analysis

- Employee attrition trends
- Department and job-role attrition
- Demographic patterns
- Compensation and income-related factors
- Overtime and business travel
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance
- Workforce retention indicators
